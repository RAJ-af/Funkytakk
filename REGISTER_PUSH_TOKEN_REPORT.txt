3. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS: WHY IT WAS FAILING BEFORE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Issue 1: Cold-Start Duplicate Race Condition
- FunkyTalkApplication.onCreate() and MainActivity.onCreate() both called
  PushNotificationManager.onColdStart(context) within 185 milliseconds of
  each other on app launch:
    18:25:41.486 E/PushNotificationManager: [COLD_START] onColdStart triggered...
    18:25:41.671 E/PushNotificationManager: [COLD_START] onColdStart triggered...
- Both dispatched scope.launch { registerCurrentToken(force=true) }, causing
  two concurrent attempts to query Google Play Services GCM/FCM back-to-back.

Issue 2: Google Play Services "TOO_MANY_REGISTRATIONS"
- When multiple token calls hit Google Play Services in rapid succession on an
  emulator or device, Google Play Services throws:
    java.io.IOException: java.util.concurrent.ExecutionException:
    java.io.IOException: TOO_MANY_REGISTRATIONS
- In previous code, FirebaseMessaging.getInstance().token.await() had no internal
  try-catch inside withTimeoutOrNull.
- The unhandled exception escaped directly to the outer attempt loop:
    "Attempt 1/3 threw exception during push token registration: IOException:
     TOO_MANY_REGISTRATIONS"
- The loop immediately retried 2 more times (3 total attempts), worsening the
  rate limit and never reaching the RPC call, completely ignoring the cached
  token already stored in SharedPreferences.
